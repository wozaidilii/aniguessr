# Photo-submission passages withheld from the privacy policy

This file is not published. It keeps the photo-submission wording removed from the live policy on 2026-10-21 so it can be restored when photo submissions ship.

Live text: `src/lib/privacy-policy-content.ts`.

Section 12 (requests for disclosure, including deletion within 30 days) was not changed.

Edits in the live policy:

- Section 2: the photo-submission feature was removed from the scope sentence.
- Section 3.4: the whole subsection was removed. The former 3.5 is now 3.4. The former 3.6 is now 3.5.
- Section 4: purpose item 4 was removed. Former items 5–9 are now 4–8.
- Section 5: the photo-submission bullet was removed.
- Section 10: the photo-metadata bullet was removed.
- Section 11: the sentence that linked to the Photo Submission Terms was removed.
- Section 13: the “or submit photos” clauses were removed. The account-registration sentences otherwise stay as they were.

Top-level sections 6–15 were not renumbered, so existing references to sections 5, 9, and 15 still match.
